✦ ABOUT THE STUDIO ✦

Welcome to Sneha’s Makeup Studio, where beauty meets artistry. Our aim is to help every client feel beautiful, confident, and comfortable for their special occasion.

From timeless bridal looks to elegant engagement makeup, party glam and pre-wedding styling, each look is planned to complement your features, outfit and event.

We focus on a polished finish, attention to detail and a personalised experience for every booking.

✧ WHY CHOOSE SNEHA’S MAKEUP STUDIO?

  • Makeup looks tailored to your occasion and personal style.
  • Bridal, engagement, party and pre-wedding options.
  • One-to-one booking enquiries and appointment planning.
  • A beauty-focused experience built around your confidence.
